## What it is

An 8-week money sprint: from zero followers, zero portfolio, zero clients to
**at least $2,000 on a $0 budget**, using AI-assisted building. The sprint
started the week of Sep 28, 2026; the 8-week mark lands around **Nov 23,
2026** — effectively the Black Friday window (personal target set earlier:
$2–4k by Black Friday).

It produced two canonical documents:

1. `The_2K_Sprint_Playbook.pdf` — the plan: three lanes, weekly targets,
   pricing, outreach cadence, and the 5-stage apprenticeship with drills.

   `../../MARKETING/2K-Sprint-Marketing-Kit/`) — every piece of marketing

no rebrand.

1. **Lane 1 — local (Charm City):** build-first sites, $2,350 / 45 days
2. **Lane 2 — gigs (Fiverr/Upwork):** cash now, first order in 2 weeks,
   portfolio + reviews compounding into local authority
3. **Lane 3 — product (AssetForge):** weekend retainer lane, gated on its
   on-device acceptance nights

## Live assets finalized by the sprint (Oct 4, 2026)

- `mikebuilds.tech` — the main landing page, one roof for every line
- Hidden audience pages — direct URL only, no links from the homepage, so each
  audience never sees the others' doors: `/plumber`, `/realtor`, `/automation`
- Branded email on the same domain

Architecture rule that governs all of it: **method transfers, information
doesn't.** One door per audience. A plumber should never land on the realtor
pitch. The homepage is the only shared surface, so it shows no niche entry
points at all.
